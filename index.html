Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official government website?

No. This is the business website of DSC Mobile Zone. The shop may assist with certain CSC or online services, but this page is not a government website and is not an endorsement by a government agency.

Is ₹1,099 the final display repair price?

No. It is an indicative starting price only. The actual estimate depends on your phone model, the part required and availability. Confirm the final price with the shop before approving any repair.

How long will a repair take?

Timing depends on diagnosis, model and parts availability. The shop will confirm an estimate after checking the device.

Will the enquiry be sent automatically?

No. The form opens WhatsApp with your enquiry filled in. Review it there and press WhatsApp’s Send button. The website does not send your details in the background.
