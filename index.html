04 / Helpful answers

Before you enquire

Is display replacement always ₹1,099?

No. ₹1,099 is a starting price for eligible models, subject to confirmation. Your phone model, compatible panel, part quality and stock determine the final quote.

How long does a repair take?

The shop needs to check the fault and parts availability before confirming a completion estimate. The website does not promise same-day repairs.

Can I request a particular display type?

Yes. Select your preference in the enquiry form. LCD, OLED and AMOLED are not interchangeable on every phone; the shop must check compatibility.

Does this website send my message automatically?

No. Continuing opens WhatsApp with the enquiry. You review it and press Send inside WhatsApp.

Is this a government website?

No. This is the business website of DSC Mobile Zone. It provides information about the shop's services and does not claim government endorsement.
