SECTION 07

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official government website?

No. This is the website of DSC Mobile Zone, a private business. We help customers with some online and CSC-type services, but this site is not run by or endorsed by any government department.

Is ₹1,099 the final price for a display repair?

No, it is an indicative starting price. The final price depends on the phone model, the part and the quality option. You approve the price before we start.

How long will my repair take?

The times shown are estimates. Some repairs need parts to be ordered. We confirm the time after checking your phone.

What happens when I press Send on the form?

WhatsApp opens with your details already typed into a message to the shop. You then press Send in WhatsApp. The website never sends anything by itself.

Can I order a custom design without visiting?

Yes. Send your enquiry, then share your photo or name text on WhatsApp. We confirm the design and price before printing.
